**Prepared for:** Founders

## 1. Executive Summary

### The Problem
- Cloud AI services require sending sensitive data to third parties, creating compliance burdens (HIPAA, GDPR, SOC2)
- Existing local AI solutions lack persistent memory - every conversation starts from zero
- Enterprise AI solutions are expensive, complex, and require cloud infrastructure

### The Solution
- **Offline Intelligence** builds stateful AI runtimes with infinite context and persistent memory
- All processing happens locally on device - zero data leaves the user's hardware
- Three-tier memory system: Hot (instant recall), Warm (summaries), Cold (full history)
- Hybrid mode: Online fallback for low-resource devices

### The Market
- Global AI software market: $150B+ (2026)
- Privacy-focused AI: Growing at 35% CAGR
- Enterprise AI on-device: $12B market by 2028

### The Ask
- **Raise:** $1-3M (Pre-seed)
- **Use of funds:** Product development, team expansion, go-to-market
- **Equity:** Flexible (10-20%)

- **Profile:** Developers, tech enthusiasts, hobbyists

- **Behavior:** Download free tools, contribute to communities, provide feedback

#### Segment B: Creative Professionals
- **Profile:** Filmmakers, designers, content creators
- **Pain:** Need AI generation tools without subscription fees

## 9. Risks and Mitigations

### Risk Matrix

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Low user adoption | Medium | High | Strong GTM, community focus |
| Competitor launches memory | Low | High | First-mover advantage, moat |
| Enterprise sales take long | High | Medium | Diversify revenue (paid products) |
| Technical challenges | Low | High | Proven tech stack |
| Runway runs out | Medium | Critical | Lean operations, revenue focus |

### Mitigation Strategies

1. **User adoption risk:**
   - Start GTM early (waitlist, content)
   - Focus on community building
   - Iterate based on feedback

2. **Enterprise sales risk:**
   - Diversify with paid consumer products
   - Build relationships early
   - Offer pilot programs

3. **Technical risk:**
   - Leverage proven tech (llama.cpp, Rust, SQLite)
   - Incremental feature development
